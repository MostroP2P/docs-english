# Can I cancel an order?

If you created an order and it hasn't been taken yet, you can cancel it. This includes an order still waiting for its [anti-abuse deposit](./anti-abuse-bond.md). In the [Mostro App](./mostro-mobile.md), open your order and confirm *Cancel the order?* with **Yes, cancel**.

If the order has been taken but the trade has not started yet — the seller has not paid the [hold invoice](./hold-invoice.md) or the buyer has not sent their invoice — either of you can cancel it directly, without the other's consent.

Once the seller's sats are locked, if you decide not to proceed you will need to initiate a cooperative cancellation with your counterparty. In the app, tap **Cancel trade**; your counterparty sees **Accept cancel**. If both parties agree, Mostro will cancel the [hold invoice](./hold-invoice.md) paid by the seller, and the sats will be returned to them. This also works while a dispute is open, and closes it.

In case your counterparty does not want to cancel, you can initiate a [dispute](./disputes.md).
